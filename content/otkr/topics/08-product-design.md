# Topic 8. Designing the Heritage Tourism Product

> **Short note** — no lecture slides yet; based on the syllabus summary. Will be expanded when the slides arrive.

Tourism as experience: this lecture shows how to build holistic, immersive and place-based heritage tourism products. It stresses emotional connection, narrative design and the integration of cultural and human elements into tour packages.

## Key definitions

- **Tourism product** – everything a visitor buys and experiences: the route, content, services, people and atmosphere together.
- **Holistic product** – a product designed as one complete experience, not a list of separate services.
- **Immersive product** – a product that lets visitors take part and feel "inside" the story, not only watch.
- **Place-based product** – a product built on what is unique to one particular place.
- **Narrative design** – organising the product around a clear story with a beginning, development and end.
- **Tour package** – a combination of services (transport, guiding, food, accommodation, activities) sold together.

## Main points

- Modern heritage tourism sells **experiences**, not only sightseeing.
- A good product creates an **emotional connection** between the visitor and the place.
- **Narrative design** links all parts of the tour into one story.
- **Cultural and human elements** – local people, crafts, food, traditions – are part of the package, not extras.
- Students review international examples (for example immersive museum tours, historical reenactments) and ask what made them effective (independent work for week 8).
- Seminar task: a **product blueprint** – a 1-day or weekend tour around a unique cultural identity, with route map, content, pricing and a promotion idea.

## How to answer in the exam

- Explain what "tourism as experience" means.
- Name the qualities of a good product: holistic, immersive, place-based.
- Explain the role of narrative in product design (exam question 10).
- Give an example structure of a tour: theme, route, content, price, promotion.
