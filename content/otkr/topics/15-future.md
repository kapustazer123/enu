# Topic 15. The Future of Heritage Tourism

> **Short note** — no lecture slides yet; based on the syllabus summary. Will be expanded when the slides arrive.

Where is heritage tourism heading? This final lecture discusses trends in climate change, digitalization, inclusivity and community empowerment, and the future challenges and opportunities of global heritage tourism.

## Key definitions

- **Trend** – a long-term direction of change that affects many destinations.
- **Climate change** – long-term change in climate that can threaten heritage sites and change travel patterns.
- **Digitalization** – the growing use of digital technologies in planning, managing, promoting and experiencing heritage.
- **Inclusivity** – making heritage accessible and meaningful for all people, regardless of ability, age or background.
- **Community empowerment** – giving local communities real control and benefits in heritage tourism.
- **Scenario planning** – imagining several possible futures in order to prepare for them.

## Main points

- The lecture names four key trends: **climate change, digitalization, inclusivity, community empowerment**.
- Each trend brings both **challenges** (risks to sites and communities) and **opportunities** (new products, new audiences).
- Scenario planning helps destinations prepare for a future that is uncertain.
- Students forecast how one global trend (for example AI, climate change, migration) could reshape tourism in their city by 2035 (independent work for week 15).
- Seminar task: a **scenario plan for 2035** based on 3 global trends, presented as a future news article or creative visual.

## How to answer in the exam

- List the four trends from the lecture and give one challenge and one opportunity for each.
- Explain scenario planning and why it helps long-term development (exam question 25).
- Be ready for questions on climate change (question 73), AI in destination management (question 74) and heritage tourism in 2035 (question 75).
- Connect to earlier topics: sustainability, community involvement, digital tools.
