# Topic 9. Marketing Strategies for Heritage Destinations

Heritage marketing communicates the value of heritage to visitors while balancing preservation, economic sustainability and visitor experience. The lecture moves from audience and storytelling to digital channels, flow management and success metrics.

## Key definitions

- **Segmentation** – dividing visitors into groups by their motivations, so that each group gets a suitable value proposition.
- **Narrative marketing** – promoting a site through human stories rather than dry facts.
- **USP (unique selling proposition)** – the unique historical and cultural value that makes a destination different.
- **UGC (user-generated content)** – photos and posts made by visitors themselves, which give organic social media reach.
- **De-seasonalization** – spreading visitor demand across times and seasons to avoid peaks.

## Main points

**1. The core dilemma of heritage marketing**

| Cultural preservation | Commercial growth |
|---|---|
| Authenticity – safeguard historical integrity | Monetization – sustainable revenue streams |
| Carrying capacity – prevent physical degradation | Audience expansion – attract value-driven visitors |
| Community respect – honour local heritage and residents | Reinvestment – secure funding for conservation |

**2. Target audience segmentation**
- **Cultural explorers** – want deep historical context and academic accuracy.
- **Experience seekers** – focus on visuals, atmosphere and social shareability.
- **Family groups** – need interactive learning, games and accessible amenities.

**3. Narrative marketing: from facts to human stories**
- Personalization – stories of historical figures.
- Mystery and discovery – revealing lesser-known narratives.
- Emotional resonance – building lasting connections with the site.

**4. Digital engagement channels** (for attraction and pre-visit engagement)
- **AR/VR reconstructions** – show lost architectural elements on mobile devices.
- **Heritage podcasts** – long-form audio storytelling for remote audiences.
- **UGC and social zones** – curated photo spots for organic reach.

**5. On-site visitor experience**
- Seamless navigation – intuitive wayfinding without congestion.
- Interactive zones – hands-on crafts and workshops.
- Authentic retail – artisan merchandise instead of mass souvenirs.

**6. Flow management and de-seasonalization**
- **Dynamic pricing** – rewards off-peak weekday and morning visits.
- **Exclusive night tours** – after-hours entry with special programmes.
- **Off-season festivals** – create demand in the low winter season.

**7. Successful strategy checklist**
- Clear **USP**.
- **Capacity controls** – timed-entry ticketing.
- **Regional synergy** – partner with local hospitality and guides.
- **Environmental respect** – minimise the tourism footprint on sites.

## Examples

- **Economic impact:** immersive audio tours and evening programmes are linked to a **+40%** average increase in visitor spending.
- **Marketing KPIs** used in the lecture:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Visitor satisfaction rate | 85% |
| Repeat visit growth | 2.5x |
| Advance online bookings | 60% |

- Seminar task: analyse a destination's marketing with the **7Ps framework** and propose an improved campaign for one segment.
