# Topic 3. Inventory and Evaluation of Cultural Heritage Resources

> **Short note** — no lecture slides yet; based on the syllabus summary. Will be expanded when the slides arrive.

How do we identify and assess heritage assets? This lecture covers creating inventories, mapping cultural landscapes, and evaluating the integrity, significance and authenticity of historical resources for tourism.

## Key definitions

- **Heritage inventory** – a systematic list of the heritage assets of an area, with a description of each one.
- **Cultural landscape** – an area shaped by the interaction of people and nature over time, seen as one whole rather than as separate objects.
- **Integrity** – how complete and intact a resource is.
- **Significance** – why a resource is important (historical, cultural, social or scientific value).
- **Authenticity** – how truthfully a resource shows its original form, materials and meaning.
- **Tangible / intangible heritage** – physical objects and places vs. living traditions, skills, stories and practices.

## Main points

- The work has three steps: **identify** assets, **map** them, **evaluate** them.
- Evaluation criteria include integrity, significance and authenticity; the seminar also uses **accessibility** (is it ready to receive visitors?).
- Assets can be classified using a **UNESCO or ICOMOS classification system** (independent work for week 3).
- The result is usually presented as a table or a map.
- Seminar task: a **cultural asset audit** of your local area – at least 10 assets, classified and evaluated.
