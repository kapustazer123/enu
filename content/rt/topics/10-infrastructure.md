# Topic 10. Recreational infrastructure: resorts, recreation bases, camps, glamping

> **Based on the course annotation and open sources** — not the teacher's lecture. Check key points against the lectures.

Recreational infrastructure is the set of buildings, facilities and services that make it possible to use recreational resources: accommodation, food, treatment, sports, transport and engineering networks. Without it, even the best natural resource cannot become a destination.

## Key definitions
- **Recreational infrastructure** — facilities and networks that serve holidaymakers: accommodation, catering, medical and sports facilities, entertainment, transport, water and energy supply, waste systems.
- **Resort hotel** — a hotel in a resort area with leisure, sports and often spa or medical services.
- **Recreation base (baza otdykha)** — a seasonal or all-year complex with simple accommodation (cottages, rooms), catering and basic leisure, usually on a lake or in the forest.
- **Children's camp** — an institution for the organised rest and recreation of children, often in summer.
- **Campsite (camping)** — a site for tents, caravans or cars with sanitary facilities.
- **Glamping** ("glamorous camping") — rest in nature in comfortable structures (domes, safari tents, tiny houses) with beds, electricity and service.

## Main points
**1. Components of recreational infrastructure**

| Group | Examples |
|---|---|
| Accommodation | Sanatoriums, resort hotels, recreation bases, guest houses, camps, campsites, glamping |
| Catering | Restaurants, cafes, canteens in sanatoriums |
| Medical and wellness | Mineral water pump rooms, mud baths, spa centres |
| Sports and leisure | Ski lifts, beaches, boat rentals, trails, aquaparks |
| Transport | Roads, airports, railway, parking, shuttle buses |
| Engineering | Water supply, sewage, electricity, internet, waste management |
| Information | Visitor centres, signage, booking websites |

**2. Types of recreational accommodation by function**
- **Medical:** sanatoriums, preventive sanatoriums (profilaktoriums), boarding houses with treatment.
- **Rest:** resort hotels, boarding houses (pansionats), recreation bases, holiday homes.
- **Youth and children:** children's camps, youth camps, hostels.
- **Tourist/nature:** campsites, tourist shelters, mountain huts, glamping.

**3. Requirements for infrastructure**
- Safety, comfort and accessibility (including people with disabilities).
- Harmony with the landscape (low-rise buildings, natural materials).
- Ecological solutions: waste water treatment, renewable energy, waste sorting.
- All-season use to reduce seasonality.

**4. Trends**
- Growth of glamping and eco-lodges in national parks and near lakes.
- Upgrading old Soviet-era recreation bases and sanatoriums.
- Development of ski infrastructure and mountain resorts.
- Online booking and digital services.

## Examples
- **Kazakhstan:**
  - Shymbulak ski resort and Medeu high-mountain skating rink near Almaty.
  - Recreation bases on the shores of Alakol, Balkhash and Kapshagay reservoir.
  - Sanatoriums and hotels of Burabay and Saryagash.
  - Glamping sites have appeared near Almaty, in the Burabay area and in the Altai.
- **World:** all-inclusive resort hotels in Turkey and Egypt; campsites in Europe; ski infrastructure in the Alps.

- Explain the difference between a recreation base, a sanatorium and glamping.
