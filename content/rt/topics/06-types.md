# Topic 6. Types of recreational activity and classification of recreational tourism

> **Based on the course annotation and open sources** — not the teacher's lecture. Check key points against the lectures.

Recreational activity takes many forms, from lying on a beach to climbing a mountain. Classification helps to plan territories, design products and study demand. Recreational tourism is classified by purpose, activity level, duration, season, environment, organisation and number of participants.

## Key definitions
- **Type of recreational activity** — a group of activities with a common aim and similar resource needs (e.g. medical, sports, educational).
- **Passive recreation** — rest with low physical activity (beach rest, sunbathing, reading in nature).
- **Active recreation** — rest with physical effort (hiking, skiing, rafting, cycling).
- **Recreational occupation** — a single activity (fishing, swimming); several occupations form a recreational cycle or programme.

## Main points
**1. Main types of recreational activity (by purpose)**
- **Medical (curative)** — treatment at resorts and sanatoriums.
- **Health-improving (wellness)** — rest and prevention: beach rest, walking, spa.
- **Sports** — hiking, mountaineering, skiing, water sports, horse riding.
- **Educational (cognitive)** — sightseeing, excursions, ecological trails.
- **Entertainment** — amusement parks, festivals, nightlife.
- **Amateur / hobby** — fishing, hunting, photography, gathering mushrooms and berries.

**2. Classification of recreational tourism**

| Criterion | Types |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Medical, health-improving, sports, educational, entertainment |
| Activity level | Passive, active, extreme |
| Duration | Short-term (1–3 days, weekend), long-term (holiday) |
| Season | Summer, winter, all-season, off-season |
| Natural environment | Beach (coastal), mountain, lake/river, forest, steppe, desert |
| Organisation | Organised (package, tour operator), independent (self-organised) |
| Participants | Individual, family, group, children's, youth, seniors |
| Geography | Domestic, inbound, outbound |
| Mode of movement | Walking, cycling, horse, water, car, ski |

**3. Special forms connected with recreation**
- **Ecotourism** — travel to natural areas with education and conservation.
- **Agritourism / rural tourism** — rest in villages and farms.
- **Adventure tourism** — activities with physical challenge and some risk.
- **Glamping and camping** — rest in nature with tents or comfortable structures.

**4. Why classification matters**
- Different types need different resources, infrastructure and safety rules.
- It helps to plan zoning, estimate carrying capacity and create target-oriented products.

## Examples
- **Beach / lake rest:** Alakol, Kapshagay reservoir, Caspian coast near Aktau.
- **Mountain sports:** skiing at Shymbulak; trekking in Ile-Alatau National Park.
- **Educational:** day trip to Charyn Canyon and Kolsai lakes from Almaty.
- **Amateur:** fishing on the Irtysh River; bird watching in Korgalzhyn Reserve (Akmola Region).
- **Agritourism:** guest houses in villages near Burabay or in the Almaty mountains.
- **World:** passive beach rest in Antalya; active skiing in the Alps.

- Present the classification criteria in a table with examples.
- Explain the difference between passive, active and extreme recreation.
