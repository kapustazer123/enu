# Topic 12. Recreational resources and resort zones of Kazakhstan

> **Based on the course annotation and open sources** — not the teacher's lecture. Check key points against the lectures.

Kazakhstan has very diverse recreational resources: high mountains of the Tien Shan and Altai, forest-lake oases in the steppe, large lakes and reservoirs, the Caspian coast, deserts, canyons and mineral springs. The state sees tourism as a priority sector and develops key resort zones.

## Key definitions
- **Resort zone** — a territory with recreational resources and concentrated infrastructure, planned for mass recreation.
- **Forest-lake oasis** — a small area of pine forest, hills and lakes inside the steppe (typical of Burabay, Bayanaul, Karkaraly).
- **Domestic tourism** — travel of residents within their own country; the main market for most Kazakhstan resorts.

## Main points
**1. Main recreational regions and destinations**

| Region | Key destinations | Main type of recreation |
|---|---|---|
| North (Akmola) | Burabay National Park, Shchuchinsk–Borovoe resort zone, Kokshetau lakes | Climatic, health, lake rest, wellness |
| North-east (Pavlodar) | Bayanaul National Park, Moiyldy | Lake rest, mud therapy |
| Centre (Karaganda) | Karkaraly National Park, Lake Balkhash | Forest-lake rest, beach rest |
| South-east (Almaty, Zhetisu) | Ile-Alatau NP, Medeu, Shymbulak, Kapshagay reservoir, Charyn, Kolsai lakes, Altyn-Emel, Alakol | Mountain, ski, beach, ecotourism |
| East (East Kazakhstan, Abai) | Altai, Katon-Karagay NP, Rakhmanov springs, Bukhtarma reservoir, Alakol (north-east shore) | Mountain, health, water rest |
| South (Turkestan, Zhambyl) | Saryagash, Aksu-Zhabagly, Sayram-Ugam NP, Turkistan | Balneological, ecotourism, cultural |
| West (Mangystau, Atyrau) | Caspian coast near Aktau, Bozzhyra, underground mosques | Beach rest, desert landscapes, pilgrimage |

**2. Strengths**
- Variety of resources in one country (from ski slopes to saline lakes and mineral springs).
- Large network of national parks and reserves.
- Growing domestic demand and new airports, roads and visa-free regimes for many countries.

**3. Problems**
- Strong seasonality: most lake and beach resorts work only in summer.
- Large distances and uneven transport access.
- Old infrastructure in many recreation bases and sanatoriums; lack of sewage and waste systems on lake shores.
- Uneven quality of service and lack of qualified staff.
- Pressure on fragile nature (litter, overcrowding at popular sites).

**4. State policy**
- The Law "On Tourist Activity in the Republic of Kazakhstan" (2001) is the main law on tourism.
- The **Concept for the Development of the Tourism Industry of the Republic of Kazakhstan for 2023–2029** (Government Decree No. 262, 28 March 2023) sets the strategy for the sector.
- Priority directions include developing Burabay, Alakol, the Almaty mountain cluster, the Caspian coast and Turkistan.

## Examples
- **Burabay** — "Kazakh Switzerland": pine forests, Lake Borovoe and Lake Shchuchye, Okzhetpes rock; sanatoriums and hotels.
- **Alakol** — saline lake on the border of Abai and Zhetisu regions; resort villages Akshi and Koktuma (south-west) and Kabanbay (north-east).
- **Balkhash** — one of the largest lakes in Asia; the western part is fresh, the eastern part saline.
- **Kapshagay reservoir** — weekend beach destination for Almaty residents (the city of Kapshagay was renamed Konaev in 2022).
- **Saryagash** — the main mineral-water resort of the country.

- Describe two or three resort zones in detail (Burabay, Alakol, Almaty mountains).
- Name problems and the state policy documents.
- Suggest ways to develop the regions (all-season products, infrastructure, ecology).
