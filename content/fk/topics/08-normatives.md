# Topic 8. Fitness Standards (Normatives) and How the Credit Works

> **Based on the course syllabus (in Kazakh) and open sources** — not the teacher's lecture.

The course ends with a **differentiated credit** (дифференциалды есеп), not a written exam. Your grade comes mainly from practical work in classes and the two midterm controls. The syllabus does **not** list the actual normative values — get them from your teacher.

## Key definitions
- **Normative (норматив)** — a fixed standard of a physical test (time, distance or number of repetitions) that gives a mark.
- **Midterm control (аралық бақылау)** — a "control-practical work" held in week 7 and week 15.
- **SRO / SÖZh** — independent student work (self-study tasks) with deadlines in weeks 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15.

## Main points
**What the syllabus says:**
1. Midterm 1 and midterm 2 are both control-practical work. In the Semester 1 plan, midterm 1 (week 7) is demonstrating a morning gymnastics complex and midterm 2 (week 15) is passing fitness standards. In the Semester 2 plan, both week 7 and week 15 are fitness standards.
2. Forms of current and midterm control are set by the teacher; the final form is set by the department.
3. Letter grades: A 95–100, A− 90–94, B+ 85–89, B 80–84, B− 75–79, C+ 70–74, C 65–69, C− 60–64, D+ 55–59, D 50–54, FX 25–49.
4. High grades require regular attendance, correct technique, keeping safety rules, good physical qualities, SRO done on time, self-monitoring and a healthy lifestyle attitude. FX is given if normatives and SRO are not completed.

**A 2–3 week preparation plan (if you are healthy and allowed to train):**

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3–4 sessions: easy running 15–20 min, technique drills, push-ups / sit-ups 2–3 sets at a comfortable level; test yourself once |
| 2 | Add 4–6 short accelerations (20–30 m), jumping practice, slightly more repetitions; one session at test pace |
| 3 | Lower the volume 2–3 days before the test; sleep well; rehearse the exact tests once |

Always warm up, measure your pulse (Topic 3), and never train through pain or illness.

**Questions to ask your teacher:**
- Which exact tests are in each midterm (for example running distances, long jump, push-ups, sit-ups, pull-ups)?
- What are the values for each mark, for men and women?
- How are the midterm, attendance and SRO combined into the final grade?
- Is there a retake date, and what about students in a special medical group?
- Which semester plan applies to our group this term?

## How to use this for the credit
- Get the normative table early and test yourself in week 1.
- Do not miss classes: attendance is part of every grade description.
