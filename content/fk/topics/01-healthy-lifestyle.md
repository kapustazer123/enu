# Topic 1. Introduction to Physical Education and a Healthy Lifestyle

> **Based on the course syllabus (in Kazakh) and open sources** — not the teacher's lecture.

Physical education (PE) is the organised process of developing the body, movement skills and health through physical exercise. A healthy lifestyle is the set of daily habits that keeps a person healthy and able to work and study well.

## Key definitions
- **Physical culture** — the part of general culture that includes physical exercise, sport, health habits and knowledge about the body.
- **Physical education** — the teaching process that develops motor skills, physical qualities and knowledge about exercise.
- **Physical qualities** — strength, speed, endurance, flexibility and agility (coordination).
- **Healthy lifestyle** — regular activity, rational nutrition, enough sleep, personal hygiene, no smoking or alcohol abuse, and a balanced work–rest routine.
- **Health** — according to the WHO, a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely the absence of disease.

## Main points
**Goals and tasks of PE at university (per the syllabus aims):**
1. Understand the theoretical basis of physical culture and how exercise affects health.
2. Choose exercises that match your age and abilities.
3. Strengthen health and raise physical fitness.
4. Apply healthy lifestyle principles in daily life.
5. Prepare for future professional work (a tourism specialist often walks, stands and travels a lot).

**Main components of a healthy lifestyle:**

| Component | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Physical activity | Regular exercise several times a week, walking, active breaks |
| Rational nutrition | Regular meals, variety, vegetables and fruit, enough water |
| Sleep and rest | A stable daily routine and enough night sleep |
| Hygiene | Body, clothes, sports shoes, clean training place |
| No harmful habits | No smoking, vaping or alcohol abuse |
| Stress control | Rest, hobbies, breathing exercises, communication |

**Basic principles of PE** (from the syllabus self-study list): activity and consciousness, visual demonstration, systematic training, gradual increase of load, individual approach, accessibility.

## Examples
- A student who sits at lectures all day takes a 5-minute active break every hour and walks home instead of taking the bus for a few stops.
- Morning gymnastics (Topic 5) is a simple daily habit that combines activity and routine.

## How to use this for the credit
- Know the five physical qualities and the main components of a healthy lifestyle.
- Be ready to explain in two or three sentences why PE matters for students.
- This theory supports your self-study tasks (SRO) on health, nutrition and PE principles.
