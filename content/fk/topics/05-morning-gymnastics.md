# Topic 5. Morning Hygienic Gymnastics

> **Based on the course syllabus (in Kazakh) and open sources** — not the teacher's lecture.

Morning hygienic gymnastics is a short set of easy exercises done after waking up. It "wakes up" the body and prepares it for the day. In the Semester 1 plan, **midterm 1 (week 7)** is choosing and demonstrating your own morning gymnastics complex.

## Key definitions
- **Morning hygienic gymnastics (таңертеңгілік гигиеналық гимнастика, ТГГ)** — 10–15 minutes of light general exercises in the morning.
- **General developmental exercises** — simple exercises for the main muscle groups and joints, without special equipment.
- **Complex** — a fixed sequence of exercises with a set number of repetitions (dosage).

## Main points
**Why it matters:** it raises heart rate and breathing gently, improves blood flow, makes joints mobile, improves mood and attention, and builds the habit of daily activity.

**Rules for building a complex:**
1. 8–12 exercises, about 10–15 minutes, moderate intensity (no heavy loads).
2. Order "from top to bottom" by body area: neck, arms and shoulders, trunk, legs; then jumps, then calm walking and breathing.
3. Begin and end with walking and breathing exercises.
4. Do it in a ventilated room or outdoors; drink some water; do not exercise to exhaustion.
5. Adapt exercises to your health: choose gentle variants if you have a medical restriction.

**Sample complex (10 exercises) you can demonstrate:**

| # | Exercise | Dosage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walking on the spot with high knees, arm swings | 1 min |
| 2 | Head tilts forward–back and side to side (slow, no full circles) | 4–6 times each way |
| 3 | Arm circles forward and backward | 8 times each way |
| 4 | Feet shoulder-width, trunk bends to the left and right, arm over head | 6–8 each side |
| 5 | Trunk twists left and right, hands on waist | 8 each side |
| 6 | Forward bend, try to touch the floor, knees straight | 8 times |
| 7 | Squats, arms forward, heels on the floor | 10–15 times |
| 8 | Lunges forward, alternating legs | 6–8 each leg |
| 9 | Push-ups (from the floor or from knees / a bench) | 8–12 times |
| 10 | Light jumps on the spot, then walking with deep breathing (arms up — breathe in, arms down — breathe out) | 20–30 jumps + 1 min |

**For the demonstration:** name each exercise, its starting position and dosage; show correct posture; count aloud; keep calm, even breathing.

## Examples
- A student with a knee problem replaces lunges with leg raises lying on the back and squats with half-squats.

## How to use this for the credit
- Learn your own complex by heart and practise it daily for 1–2 weeks before week 7.
- Be ready to explain why the order goes from top to bottom.
- Ask your teacher whether you must write the complex on paper or only show it.
