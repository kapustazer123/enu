# Topic 6. Outdoor Games, Kazakh National Games, Sports Games and Relays

> **Based on the course syllabus (in Kazakh) and open sources** — not the teacher's lecture.

Movement games develop speed, agility, endurance and teamwork in an emotional, playful way. The syllabus (weeks 8–11) includes outdoor games, Kazakh national movement games played in groups, sports games and relay races built from athletics elements.

## Key definitions
- **Movement (outdoor) game** — a game with simple rules where running, jumping, throwing or catching are the main actions.
- **National games** — traditional games of a people that reflect its history and way of life.
- **Sports game** — a game with official rules and competitions (football, volleyball, basketball, handball).
- **Relay race (эстафета)** — a team competition where participants run or do tasks in turn, passing a baton or touching the next player.

## Main points
**Effect on health:** games raise heart rate and breathing, develop physical qualities, improve reaction and coordination, reduce stress, and teach cooperation, fair play and leadership.

**Well-known Kazakh national games:**

| Game | Short description |
|---|---|
| Asyk atu | Players shoot a sheep's knucklebone (asyk) at a row of other asyks to knock them out; develops accuracy and finger strength |
| Arkan tartys | Tug of war with a rope between two teams |
| Kazakh kuresi | Traditional Kazakh belt wrestling |
| Aksuyek ("white bone") | A night team game: a white bone is thrown far away and teams race to find it and bring it back |
| Kokpar | Horseback team game where riders fight for a goat carcass and carry it to the goal; a national equestrian sport |
| Kyz kuu ("catch the girl") | Horseback chase game between a young man and a young woman |
| Audaryspak | Wrestling on horseback |
| Tenge alu | Picking up a coin or scarf from the ground at full gallop |

In a university gym, games like asyk atu, arkan tartys and team tasks are realistic; horseback games are studied as national heritage.

**Relays in class:** shuttle runs, running with a baton, relays with jumps or ball passing. Rules: start only after a touch or baton pass, stay in your lane, return equipment to its place.

**Main sports games (also listed as post-requisites in the syllabus):** football, volleyball, basketball, handball.

## Examples
- A class relay: 4 teams, each runner sprints 20 m, goes round a cone and passes the baton.

## How to use this for the credit
- Participate actively: the grading criteria value activity and teamwork.
- Be able to name and briefly describe 3–4 Kazakh national games.

**Facts to check:** local rules of the games your teacher uses in class.
