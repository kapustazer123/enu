# Topic 7. Athletics: Running and Standing Long Jump

> **Based on the course syllabus (in Kazakh) and open sources** — not the teacher's lecture.

In weeks 12–14 the syllabus covers middle-distance running, short-distance (sprint) running and the standing long jump. These are also typical events for fitness standards, so good technique directly improves your results.

## Key definitions
- **Short distances (sprints)** — 60 m, 100 m, 200 m, 400 m; maximum speed is the main quality.
- **Middle distances** — 800 m and 1500 m (in PE tests also 1000 m); speed endurance is the main quality.
- **Low start** — sprint start from crouching position, hands on the line.
- **High (standing) start** — start from a standing position, used in middle-distance running.
- **Circuit training (шеңберлік жаттығулар)** — several exercise stations done one after another.

## Main points
**Sprint technique — four phases:**
1. **Start:** commands "On your marks" — "Set" — signal. On "Set", hips rise slightly above shoulders.
2. **Acceleration:** first steps short and powerful, body leaning forward, gradually straightening.
3. **Running at distance:** run on the balls of the feet, high knees, arms bent about 90° moving forward–back (not across the body).
4. **Finish:** do not slow down before the line; lean the chest forward at the finish.

**Middle-distance technique:**
- High start, then calm acceleration; choose an even pace you can keep.
- Body nearly upright, relaxed shoulders, shorter stride than in sprint.
- Rhythmic breathing (for example, in for 2 steps, out for 2 steps).
- Save energy for the final spurt in the last 100–200 m.

**Standing long jump — phases:**
1. **Preparation:** feet shoulder-width behind the line; swing arms back, bend knees, lean forward.
2. **Take-off:** strong push from both feet together, arms swing forward and up.
3. **Flight:** pull knees forward towards the chest.
4. **Landing:** land on both feet, bend knees, move arms and body forward so you do not fall back.

The result is usually measured from the take-off line to the nearest mark left by the body (normally the heels), so falling back reduces your result.

**Special running exercises:** skipping with high knees, heel kicks, straight-leg running, bounding strides, short accelerations.

## Examples
- A circuit: high-knee skipping 20 m, squat jumps ×10, push-ups ×10, plank 30 s, then rest and repeat.

## How to use this for the credit
- Practise starts and the arm swing for the jump; technique gives quick gains.
- Always warm up well before sprints and jumps (Topic 2).
