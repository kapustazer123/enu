# Topic 2. Safety in PE Classes and Injury Prevention

> **Based on the course syllabus (in Kazakh) and open sources** — not the teacher's lecture.

Most sports injuries in PE classes can be prevented by correct clothing, a good warm-up, discipline and following the teacher's instructions. The syllabus covers causes of injuries, ways to prevent them, and requirements for training places.

## Key definitions
- **Safety rules (техника безопасности)** — rules of behaviour that protect students from injury during classes.
- **Warm-up** — light exercises at the start of a class that raise body temperature and heart rate and prepare muscles and joints.
- **Medical group** — the group a student is assigned to after a medical check (commonly basic, preparatory or special); it sets the allowed load.

## Main points
**Common causes of injuries:**
1. No warm-up or a too short warm-up.
2. Wrong technique or too heavy a load for the student's level.
3. Unsuitable clothing or shoes, jewellery, watches, chewing gum.
4. Breaking discipline: running without a signal, pushing, playing with equipment.
5. Poor conditions: slippery floor, bad lighting, broken equipment.
6. Training while ill or very tired.

**Typical PE injuries:** bruises, abrasions, sprains of the ankle or wrist, muscle strains, and in rare cases fractures or dislocations.

**Prevention:**
- Wear sports clothes and non-slip sports shoes; remove jewellery.
- Always warm up; finish with a cool-down and stretching.
- Tell the teacher about any health problem or how you feel today.
- Start exercises only on the teacher's signal; keep a safe distance in running and throwing.
- Check equipment before use; do not hang on goals or wall bars without permission.

**Requirements for training places:** a clean, dry, non-slip floor; good lighting and ventilation; safe, fixed equipment; a first-aid kit; free space around play areas.

**First aid for a sprain or bruise (widely used "RICE" rule):** Rest, Ice (cold, wrapped in cloth), Compression (elastic bandage), Elevation. Tell the teacher at once; see a doctor if pain is strong or the joint looks deformed.

## Examples
- Running in socks or smooth shoes on a gym floor can cause slips and ankle sprains.
- In relay races, runners must pass on the correct side to avoid collisions.

## How to use this for the credit
- Follow safety rules in every class: the syllabus grading criteria mention keeping safety rules.
- Be able to name 3–4 causes of injury and 3–4 prevention measures.
- Know the RICE rule.
