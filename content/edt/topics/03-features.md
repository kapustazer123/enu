# Topic 3. Peculiarities of excursion service

Excursion service is a specialised segment of tourism. Unlike accommodation, transport or food services, it focuses on interpretation, education and experiential contact with the destination, so its success depends mostly on content, the guide and careful organisation.

## Key definitions

- **Excursion service** – the provision of informative, culturally enriching and emotionally engaging experiences through guided visits to sites of interest.
- **Dramaturgy of an excursion** – the planned structure of the route: clear theme, logical transitions, timing and rhythm, so that the visit becomes a story.
- **Excursion script** – the prepared text of the excursion, adapted to the audience (age, culture, interests, language).
- **Guide as "emotional conductor"** – the guide is both an educator and the person who sets the mood and tone of the experience.

## Main points

**Main peculiarities of excursion service:**

1. **Dependence on content delivery** – the core is the narrative, the guide's knowledge, storytelling and communication skills, not just physical access to sights. A weak guide can spoil even a great attraction.
2. **Planning and dramaturgy** – not a random set of facts and places, but a route with a theme, transitions, timing; the script is tailored to the audience.
3. **Time-limited and mobile nature** – usually a few hours, with the group moving between sites; needs logistics (transport, site access, crowd management, schedule) and quick decisions (e.g. a closed street, bad weather).
4. **Legal and ethical framework** – licences or certification, rules on group size, noise, environmental impact and treatment of cultural property.
5. **Emotional and psychological dimension** – evoking wonder, pride, empathy, contemplation; sensitive topics need care.
6. **High interpersonal interaction** – building rapport, managing expectations, answering questions, handling illness, fatigue or complaints; intercultural communication with diverse groups.
7. **Customisation and thematic diversity** – art tours, culinary walks, architecture, pilgrimages, political heritage routes, ghost tours; each needs its own knowledge, style, even costume or props.
8. **Technology integration** – audio guides, AR, VR, GPS apps; more accessibility, multiple languages, flexible schedule, but questions about the future role of human guides.

**Quality criteria of excursion service:** accuracy of information, clarity of delivery, guide's enthusiasm, relevance of content, group management, overall satisfaction. Feedback tools: online reviews, surveys, real-time ratings. Operators invest in training, customer-service workshops and scenario-based learning.

**Competences required:** scholarly knowledge, storytelling, organisational precision, emotional intelligence, cultural competence, technological adaptability.

## Examples

- **Kazakhstan:** certified guides must complete special training and show knowledge of history, culture and foreign languages before leading groups.
- **Almaty:** urban heritage excursions about Soviet architecture, local legends and cultural revival.
- Japan: guiding is strictly monitored for cultural sensitivity and historical accuracy.
- Schoolchildren vs. international professionals vs. elderly visitors – different tone and content.
- Battlefields and genocide memorials – deep emotions, ethical reflection.
- London, New York, Istanbul – intercultural communication in multicultural cities.
- Prague – medieval-themed tours with guides in historical costume.
- Amsterdam, Singapore – self-guided smartphone app tours with 3D reconstructions.
- Paris "Paris of the Impressionists"; Cape Town township excursions.

## How to answer in the oral exam

- Define excursion service and contrast it with other tourist services (accommodation, transport, food).
- Name and briefly explain 5–6 peculiarities: content/guide, dramaturgy, time-limited and mobile, legal framework, emotional side, interpersonal interaction.
- Add customisation and technology as modern features.
- Mention quality criteria and feedback tools.
- Give the Kazakhstan examples (guide certification, Almaty heritage tours).
